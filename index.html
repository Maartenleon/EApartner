Why enterprise architecture

Two kinds of alignment.
One discipline.

Architecture creates the structure that lets organisations grow, transform, and automate — without losing coherence. It works on two axes at once.

Vertical alignment

From strategy to technology

Every strategic choice should trace a clear line down through capabilities, data, applications, and technology. When these layers are connected, decisions at the top become executable at the bottom — and constraints at the bottom feed back up before they become surprises.

Horizontal alignment

Across domains

Most complexity isn't inside a domain — it lives in the seams between them. Shared capabilities, data ownership, integration patterns, reusable platforms. Architecture creates the coherence across domains that prevents every team from solving the same problem differently.

The result: an organisation that controls complexity instead of being controlled by it — while staying ready for growth, transformation, and an increasingly automated future.

Approach
