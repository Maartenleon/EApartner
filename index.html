Why enterprise architecture

Every satisfying organisational experience, product, or process, is held together by architecture most people never see.

The pattern is familiar: too many initiatives, too little focus. An IT landscape that tangles as the organisation grows. Strategic direction that doesn't reach delivery. Delivery that drifts without strategic direction. The need isn't more technology. It's coherence across the whole.

Architecture earns its place close enough to strategy to shape decisions, and close enough to delivery to govern what actually gets built. That means two things need to work:
transformations need solid architecture to steer them, and the architecture function itself needs to deliver. How that looks depends on where you are.

What I offer

Services

Two tracks. One for organisations with complex transformations. One for organisations who want to improve their architecture function.

Track 1

Architecture for Transformation

Your transformation needs a spine: from strategy through capabilities, data, applications, and technology. Architecture that connects layers and enables decisions.

Best for: Organisations mid-transformation where decisions are stuck, unclear, or drifting

  • A clear map of where complexity sits and which decisions are urgent
  • A layered target architecture that's built to last and executable
  • A sequenced roadmap where every step traces to a decision, not a deadline
  • Embedded guidance until the path forward stays obvious without me

Track 2

Architecture as a Function

Your EA function exists but doesn't deliver influence, governance, decisions, or value. A concrete plan to make architecture stick by enhancing six capabilities.

Best for: Organisations with an EA function that exists but doesn't deliver strategic value

  • An honest baseline across all six EA capabilities, scored and compared
  • A named diagnosis of where the function loses influence and why
  • A concrete, sequenced improvement plan anchored to impact
  • Coaching support for leads and teams during the build-out
