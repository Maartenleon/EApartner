Track 1

Architecture
for Transformation

Architecture that gives complex change a spine — from an integrative long-term target to the first executable step. Built for an intelligent, agentic future.

Sound familiar?

How do I add new systems without creating an integration spaghetti down the line?

Without architecture guardrails, every new system becomes a new integration point. I define integration principles, a canonical data model, and a target architecture that new systems slot into — before they're built, not after the damage is done.

How do I decide which tech initiatives to prioritise when everything feels urgent?

Most organisations have more initiatives than focus. I map your capability gaps to strategic goals and use that as the basis for sequencing — so every initiative you fund has a clear architectural reason for coming before the others.

Which of our processes are actually suitable for AI, and which ones aren't ready yet?

AI readiness is architectural: it depends on data quality, capability ownership, and integration maturity. I assess your landscape layer by layer and give you a clear picture of where agents can deliver value today — and where the foundational work comes first.

Every programme creates its own architecture — how do we get coherence without a central bottleneck?

When architecture decisions are made programme by programme, coherence only comes from luck or conflict. I establish shared principles and a reference architecture that programmes inherit — so alignment happens by design, not constant negotiation.

The model

Vertical and
horizontal alignment

Architecture connects strategy to execution layer by layer (top to bottom), and keeps every business domain coherent with the same architecture (left to right). AI and autonomous agents operate across all layers as a cross-cutting concern.

Six layers

Strategy
to AI

Each layer informs the next. Architecture makes the connections explicit — and keeps them coherent as the organisation changes.

01

Strategy

Business intent, principles, and architectural boundaries that govern every decision below. Strategic principles that set real constraints — and a North Star that outlasts individual projects.

02

Capabilities

What the organisation does, independent of how it does it. A capability map with domain ownership, maturity heat maps, and change prioritisation anchored to capability gaps.

03

Data

Ownership, systems of record, and semantic clarity that makes integration trustworthy — and AI reliable. A conceptual data model with quality definitions and acceptance criteria.

04

Applications

The systems that enable capabilities. An application landscape that surfaces redundancy, integration complexity, and the right modernisation sequence before the wrong one gets funded.

05

Technology

The foundation. Platform choices constrain everything above. A technology radar, build-vs-buy principles, and debt mapping that makes the right bets visible before they become irreversible.

06

AI / Agents

Agentic systems operate across all five layers as a cross-cutting concern. Architecture must specify where autonomous action is sanctioned — what agents can access, trigger, and decide, and where oversight is required.
