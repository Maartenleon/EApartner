Track 1

Architecture
for Transformation

Architecture that gives complex change a spine — from an integrative long-term target to the first executable step. Built for an intelligent, agentic future.

The model

Vertical and
horizontal alignment

Architecture connects strategy to execution layer by layer (top to bottom), and keeps every business domain coherent with the same architecture (left to right). AI and autonomous agents operate across all layers as a cross-cutting concern.

Five layers

Strategy
to technology

Each layer informs the one below. Architecture makes the connections explicit — and keeps them coherent as the organisation changes.

01

Strategy

Business intent, principles, and architectural boundaries that govern every decision below. Architecture starts where strategy meets structure.

  • Strategic principles that set real boundaries
  • Business goals translated into architectural intent
  • North Star: one-pager + scope + explicit non-goals

02

Capabilities

What the organisation does, independent of how it does it. The Rosetta Stone of EA — translating between business intent and application reality.

  • Capability model (L1/L2) with domain ownership
  • Heat maps: maturity, investment, strategic alignment
  • Change prioritisation anchored to capability gaps

03

Data

Ownership, systems of record, and the semantic clarity that makes integration trustworthy — and that makes AI reliable.

  • Data ownership model per capability domain
  • Conceptual data model + system-of-record mapping
  • Data quality definitions and acceptance criteria

04

Applications

The systems that enable capabilities. Application landscape maps which systems serve which capabilities — surfacing redundancy, gaps, and integration complexity.

  • Application landscape + capability-to-app mapping
  • Integration architecture and API inventory
  • Modernisation sequencing with dependency mapping

05

Technology

The foundation. Platform choices constrain everything above — and most technology debt lives here. About making the right bets at the right moment.

  • Technology radar + platform decision log
  • Build vs buy principles with rationale
  • Technology debt mapping and remediation sequencing

In an agent-first world

The five layers become more important, not less. Agents need clarity that humans can work around. Strategy must specify where autonomous action is sanctioned. Capabilities define what agents are responsible for. Data governs what they access. Applications determine what they can trigger. Technology provides the substrate they run on.

The layered model is not a constraint on AI. It is the structure that makes AI safe to deploy at scale.
